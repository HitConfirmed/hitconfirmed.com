Vetted ad-partner shortlist for the rewarded-video monetization lever in [[vc-phase-monetization-benchmarks]] (Path A = web rewarded video now; Path B = wrap as app later). Compliance gate overlaps [[ontario-affiliate-advertising-gate]]; positioning constraints from [[us-virtual-currency-guardrails]].

**KEY REFRAME (corrects the old "AdSense bans gambling" assumption):** AdSense/AdMob ban *real-money* gambling without a license. They have a SEPARATE allowed-but-restricted lane: "**social casino / simulated gambling**" = simulated gambling-style games with NO opportunity to win something of value. MoneyMatch's non-redeemable "Fight Money" fits this lane. Requires: Google Ads social-casino certification, on-screen disclaimer ("not real-money gambling, no prizes"), age-gate to legal gambling age, no real-money-brand logos. **Canada is an allowed country.** Source: Google Gambling & Games publisher policy (support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15132179), Oct 2025 update that pushed sweepstakes casinos OUT of this lighter lane — but pure non-redeemable social casino STAYS in it.

**Web shortlist (Path A) — accept gaming/social-casino, realistic for a browser app:**
- **AdSense H5 Games Ads** — Google's HTML5-game rewarded/interstitial product. Plain-JS embed (Ad Placement API), no hard traffic minimum, rewarded supported. MUST go through social-casino certification + disclaimers or it trips the gambling filter. Rev: standard AdSense ~68% pub share, $100 min, net ~30. Best least-work first dollar IF cert clears.
- **Playwire** — gaming-focused ad management. Verticals incl. Gaming/Casual Gaming/Entertainment. Practical bar ~500k monthly pageviews for full managed service (some sources 100k); too high for brand-new site. Net terms/rev-share not public — sales call. Rewarded via managed setup. Real-money-gambling policy not public — confirm directly.
- **Venatus** (owns **AdinPlay** since 2023) — premium gaming/sports/entertainment network, browser-game + rewarded video native. Rev share ~60-80% pub, $100 min, **Net 90** (slow pay). High-quality/high-traffic bar — not for a brand-new low-traffic site. AdinPlay = its browser-game arm, same demand.
- **Offerwall providers (AdGate Media, Lootably, AyeT Studios, Adscend, CPX Research)** — rewarded "complete-an-offer-for-currency" walls, CPA model, accept incentivized/gaming traffic, low approval bar, good fit for a "earn Fight Money" placement. Confirm each one's stance on a betting-THEMED host app directly (policies don't isolate it publicly). Offerwalls monetize via user actions, not video — complementary to, not a substitute for, rewarded video.
- **GameMonetize / CrazyGames** = game-distribution PORTALS (host your game on THEIR domain, content review incl. gambling). GameMonetize ~$1.5-3 per 1000 views, PayPal/USDT, ~25-day pay. NOT a clean fit for a standalone branded betting web app — flagged, not recommended as primary.

**Mobile shortlist (Path B, later):** AppLovin MAX + Unity LevelPlay (ex-ironSource) = the rewarded-video duopoly, highest eCPM, both serve casino/social-casino inventory (casino was fastest-growing advertiser segment 2025, ~47% advertiser share). AdMob = huge fill but strict gambling review; social-casino lane allowed w/ cert. eCPM rewarded tier-1 ~$15-40 (US Android $16.49 / iOS $19.63 Q4'24); CA slightly below US.

**App-store hurdle (Path B):** Apple Guideline 5.3 + 2025 age-rating overhaul → simulated gambling forced to 17+/18+ even with NO real money; "intense/frequent simulated gambling" label; all apps must refresh ratings by 2026-01-31. Google Play similar; real-money-gambling apps need licensing+geo-restriction (N/A to non-redeemable, but the simulated-gambling age-gate still applies).

**Recommendation logged:** Start with **AdSense H5 Games Ads via the social-casino lane** = least-work path to first measurable web dollar for a solo dev (no traffic minimum, plain-JS embed into the CRA app, Google-scale fill). Pair with **one offerwall (AdGate or Lootably)** for an additional "earn Fight Money" surface. Defer Playwire/Venatus until traffic clears ~500k monthly pageviews. Approval timeline: AdSense account review days-to-2wks + social-casino cert review; offerwall approval often faster (days). Single point of failure: a Google social-casino-cert rejection or later policy tightening — keep an offerwall as the non-Google fallback.

**Ontario/AGCO ad gotcha:** the RESTRICTION isn't only on MoneyMatch's content — it's WHAT ADS GET SHOWN. If the rewarded inventory serves Ontario-licensed sportsbook/casino ads, AGCO Std 2.05 inducement rules + affiliate-registration concerns ([[ontario-affiliate-advertising-gate]]) can attach. Age-gate (Apple/Google + ad-partner social-casino cert both require it) doubles as responsible-gaming posture. Running gambling-STYLE content can lower ad-partner acceptance regardless of "fake money" framing — the social-casino certification + disclaimer is what keeps it in the allowed lane; without it the filter treats it as prohibited real-money gambling.
