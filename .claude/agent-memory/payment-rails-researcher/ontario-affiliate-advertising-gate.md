The biggest compliance gate on the affiliate sportsbook handoff lever (see [[vc-phase-monetization-benchmarks]]). Sits alongside the Canada landmarks in [[canada-regulatory-landmarks]].

**Fact:** AGCO **Standard 2.05** prohibits public advertising of **inducements, bonuses, credits** (incl. targeted/algorithmic ads). Bonus offers may only be surfaced AFTER a user chooses to visit the operator's site, or via direct marketing to users who first consented on the gaming site.

**Affiliate registration:** Operators are responsible for ensuring their marketing affiliates meet AGCO standards; affiliates advertising for the Ontario market must not also advertise unregistered sites and effectively must be AGCO-registered/compliant.

**Enforcement is real:** AGCO fined **BetMGM Canada $110,000 (March 2025)**, partly over an affiliate that paid a **$100 cash inducement** generating $127k in commissions.

**Why:** This directly shapes how MoneyMatch's "Place on [Book]" CTA can be built — it must NOT dangle a bonus/inducement, and the operator demo is solo Canada-based (see [[operator-profile]]) so registration/liability exposure matters.

**How to apply:** Before signing any Ontario-licensed book or going live with the handoff, get Canadian gaming counsel to (1) confirm the CTA is inducement-free, (2) determine whether MoneyMatch must register as a marketing affiliate, (3) check Bill S-211 (federal gambling-ad standardization, advanced Senate Oct 2025) which may move the goalposts. Flag this as a single point of failure: non-compliant placement = loss of the affiliate line + enforcement spillover.
